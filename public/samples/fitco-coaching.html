DIRECT CLARITY

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

We eliminate guesswork before you ever step into the facility. Every partnership starts with zero commitment, zero pressure, and a candid blueprint tailored strictly to your schedule and physical baseline.

What if I am completely out of shape or brand new to strength training? +

Most of our clients come to us after years away from consistent exercise. We begin with a comprehensive joint mobility and movement baseline screen, calibrating weights and intensities strictly to your current ability so you progress safely without injury.

How many days per week do I realistically need to train for real results? +

For 90% of busy professionals, 3 high-intent, 50-minute strength sessions per week yield transformative strength gains and body composition changes when paired with our nutrition habits.

Do you provide rigid meal plans or realistic nutritional coaching? +

We reject miserable crash diets. We engineer flexible macronutrient targets and behavioral habit systems that allow you to travel, attend business dinners, and enjoy social life while sustaining leanness.

Can I train hybrid or online if my travel schedule is erratic? +

Yes. We offer fully integrated hybrid setups where your in-studio sessions sync seamlessly with customized hotel gym programming via our coaching portal when traveling.

What actually happens during the 1-on-1 strategy consultation? +

A 30-minute private deep dive: we analyze your current training roadblocks, perform a non-invasive movement assessment, and hand you a personalized physical roadmap whether you join Forge or not. Zero sales pressure.

Where is the private studio located and what parking is available? +

We are located at 2417 Westlake Drive in Austin, TX. Our private facility offers reserved gated parking and complimentary valet so arriving for early morning or evening sessions is completely friction-free.
