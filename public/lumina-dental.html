Patient Education & Clarity

Your Questions, Answered

We offer a complete comfort menu: topical numbing before any injection, noise-canceling headphones, weighted blankets, and the ability to signal a pause at any moment. Your autonomy and emotional safety come before any clinical procedure, full stop.
Our dedicated Patient Concierge handles all insurance pre-determinations and out-of-network benefit verification before your visit. We accept most PPO plans and offer third-party financing through CareCredit and Proceed Finance for complete flexibility.
Your first visit is entirely focused on listening and discovery — no treatment is performed. You will receive a full digital imaging set, a detailed oral wellness review, and a written blueprint of our findings with zero obligation to proceed with anything until you are fully ready.
Absolutely. We provide itemized, written fee estimates before any treatment begins and offer multi-month interest-free financing plans. You will never encounter surprise charges — our philosophy is complete financial transparency at every step.
We use iTero digital intraoral scanning for impressionless, mess-free captures with same-day 3D previews. Our imaging suite includes CBCT low-dose cone-beam CT scanning for complete diagnostic accuracy with up to 80% less radiation than traditional X-ray systems.
