Client Spotlight · Margaret, Age 82

She Wanted To Stay Home.

At 82, Margaret's Scottsdale home of 43 years was filled with a lifetime of cherished memories. Following a mild fall and increasing fatigue, her daughter Sarah faced a difficult crossroads: uproot her mother to an assisted facility or find trustworthy, compassionate in-home support.

Serene Haven created a personalized daily routine centered on Margaret's independence. We paired her with a certified caregiver who provides gentle morning mobility assistance, nutritious meal preparation, and thoughtful companionship tailored around Margaret's love of gardening and classic literature.

Today, Margaret continues to wake up in her own bedroom, tending her courtyard roses with confidence. Her family enjoys genuine peace of mind knowing she receives dedicated, respectful care in the place she loves most.
